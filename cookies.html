Cookie Policy

What this website stores in your browser, and why.

Short version

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site only stores two small settings in your browser, and neither identifies you.

What is stored

  • ec-theme (local storage): remembers whether you chose the night or day theme. Kept until you clear it.
  • ec-cat (session storage): remembers that the loading screen has already played so it does not repeat on every page. Removed when you close the tab.

Third parties

Google Fonts serves the typefaces and may log your IP address and browser details when it does. Links to Discord and GitHub open their own sites, which have their own cookie policies.

Managing storage

You can view and delete stored data in your browser settings. If you do, the site simply falls back to its defaults.

Changes

If we add analytics or other services later, we will update this page and ask for consent where the law requires it.
