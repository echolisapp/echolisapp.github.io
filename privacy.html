Privacy Policy

How Echolis Studio handles information on this website.

Short version

This website does not have accounts, forms or tracking, and we do not sell or share personal data. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data ourselves. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which can also see your IP address when it serves them.

What is stored in your browser

The site saves your theme choice (ec-theme) in local storage and a flag that the loading screen has already played (ec-cat) in session storage. Neither contains personal data. See the Cookies page for details.

Links to other services

Links to Discord, GitHub and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies. If you message us on Discord, we see what you send and your public Discord profile, and we use it only to answer you.

Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser at any time. To ask what we hold about you or to have a message deleted, contact us.

Changes

The site is currently in development, so this policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this page? Message us on Discord or write to hello@echolis.studio.
