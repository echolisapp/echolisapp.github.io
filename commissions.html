Commissions

Work you can hire Echolis Studio for.

What we take on

  • Minecraft mods and plugins (Fabric client mods and server plugins)
  • Thumbnails, channel banners and Minecraft renders
  • Logos, brand identity and website design
  • Desktop tools and interfaces, including voice and AI assistants

How it works

  1. Message us on Discord with what you need, any references, and your deadline.
  2. We reply with whether we can take it, plus a scope, price and timeline.
  3. Work starts once we both agree on those terms.
  4. You get drafts to review, then the final files.

Pricing and payment

Every project is priced by its scope. We agree the price and payment method with you before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Revisions and rights

Each quote states how many revisions are included. Unless agreed otherwise, you receive the rights to use the finished work, and we may show it in our portfolio.
